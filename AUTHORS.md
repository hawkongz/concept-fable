# Authors

## Creator

**hawkongz** — [@hawkongz](https://github.com/hawkongz)

Concept Fable was created by hawkongz, inspired by Amanda Askell's fable-based concept explanation technique shared in her April 2025 interview. The skill expands Askell's original prompt into a structured 7-step workflow with anti-pattern detection, quality self-checks, and cross-cultural metaphor design.

## Inspiration

**Amanda Askell** — Anthropic's in-house philosopher and head of the Character Alignment team. Her original insight — "ask AI to write a fable that explains a concept without ever naming it" — is the foundation this skill is built upon.

> *"Sometimes I can't remember the concept's formal name anymore — but I still remember the story."* — Amanda Askell
